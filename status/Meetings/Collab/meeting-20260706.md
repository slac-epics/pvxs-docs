live long term.

### To present (from 2-Jul process meeting)

**Documentation types:**

- Minutes of meetings.
- General how-to guides.
- Specific lab experiences and setup examples.
- Documents that may eventually land in normative locations (e.g. pvxs, epics-controls) but may
  be developed here first - e.g. the SPVA interfaces doc, destined for the epics-docs GitHub org,
  may be developed in secure-epics.
- Protocol specs: **link to the community items** (epics-docs) rather
  than duplicate them here (see status/ISSUES.md, pvxs-docs #5).

**Interim home:**

- **Rename `pvxs-docs` to `secure-epics`**, emphasising its role for the
  EPICS-security ecosystem and the **non-normative** docs on using the normative products (pvxs,
  phoebus). `secure-epics` becomes the interim home for guides, lab experiences, meeting minutes,
  and in-development documents.

**Long-term home and structure:**

- Normative content moves to its normative home (e.g. epics-docs GitHub org, pvxs); non-normative
  content remains in `secure-epics`. A near-term working item is to go through the existing
  `pvxs-docs` / `secure-epics` content and decide, per document, where it should live.

**Ownership and migration path:**

- `secure-epics` (renamed repo) owns the interim content and the migration; protocol specs
  migrate to community docs (link, don't duplicate); the current SPVA docs remain the reference
  until their final home is found.
